About Kretivco · Since 2018

Built from the ground up.

Kretivco wasn't born from big investment or seminar-room theory. It grew out of two entrepreneurs building something real, from nothing.

One sees what's possible before anyone else does. The other makes sure it actually happens. We still work that way with every client. We know your challenges because we've been through them ourselves.

Founders

Co-Founder & CEO / Visionary builder

Amirul Hafiz

He sold erasers to classmates at seven. Brooches in secondary school. Food on campus, then his own design studio, PakuDesign, while still at university. The pattern never changed: spot the opportunity early, then act on it.

“Every big thing starts from something small.”

Co-Founder & Operations Director / Execution architect

Nurfadilah (Ila)

She grew up helping run the family business: processes, sales and the real work of getting things delivered. At Kretivco, she's the reason plans turn into things that actually happen, on time and done properly.

“Quality comes from systems.”
