01 / KretivPOS

A point of sale that runs a real kitchen.

KretivPOS runs the counter at Sajian Baginda every day. Cashiers take orders on a simple screen, and the owner sees the numbers on a dashboard.

For the cashier

  • Tap the menu to add items
  • Change quantities and apply a discount
  • Check out and print a receipt

For the owner

  • Daily sales and number of orders
  • Cash and QR/DuitNow split
  • Best sellers, peak hours, 7-day trend
  • A

    Open and close the day

    Opening cash, expected cash and the actual count, so the drawer always adds up.

  • B

    More than one brand

    Track sales by source, like a second product line, from the same counter.

  • C

    Laptop, tablet or phone

    Use whatever device is already at the counter.
